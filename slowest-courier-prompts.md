# The Slowest Courier — prompt pack

A 30-second vertical (9:16) narrated story in six 5-second shots.

Suggested models on Higgsfield (these are the ones your trial covers):
- Images: **Nano Banana 2.1** (or Nano Banana Pro), 2K
- Video: **Seedance 2.5**, 720p, 5 seconds, 9:16, start image = the scene still, audio off
- Voice: **Text to Speech V2**, one calm, warm storyteller voice for every line

Go in this order: 1) character sheet, 2) six scene stills (attach the character sheet to each), 3) animate each still, 4) record the six narration lines, 5) put it together in CapCut.

---

## 1. Character sheet (16:9)

> Character reference sheet for an original 3D animated movie character, polished stylized 3D animation look, soft global illumination, clean light grey studio background. Character: Milo, a small friendly garden snail who works as a courier. Big round expressive eyes on short eye stalks, warm olive-green soft body, glossy spiral shell in caramel and cream stripes, a small yellow courier cap with a tiny red lightning-bolt patch, a brown leather messenger strap across the shell holding a small cardboard parcel. Show him four times in a row at the same scale: front view, three-quarter view, side profile, back view. Below, a row of four small head close-ups with different expressions: determined, tired, surprised, joyful. Consistent design, colors and proportions in every view. No text, no labels.

Generate a few and keep the one you like best. Attach it as a reference to every image below.

---

## 2. Scene stills (9:16, attach the character sheet)

Start every prompt with this line so the look stays the same:

> Polished stylized 3D animated movie still, soft warm lighting, vertical 9:16 frame. Milo the snail courier exactly as in the reference image (yellow cap, caramel striped shell, parcel on a strap).

**Shot 1 — the slow courier**
> Low angle at sidewalk level on a busy, sunny city street. Milo inches along the pavement looking determined, while electric scooters, a delivery drone and hurrying feet blur past him with motion streaks. Tall buildings behind.

**Shot 2 — always late**
> A front doorstep. Milo, out of breath, holds up the parcel. A tall impatient fox in a cardigan looks down at him, tapping a wristwatch. Autumn leaves and a calendar on the door show many days crossed off.

**Shot 3 — the big race**
> A busy city square. Milo looks up in awe at a huge banner over the street reading "THE GREAT CITY DASH — $1,000,000". Other animals point at him and laugh in the background.

**Shot 4 — the start line**
> A race start line with a crowd of animal spectators in the stands. Lined up: a sleek cheetah in running shoes, a rabbit on rocket roller skates, and tiny Milo at the end with his parcel. A referee frog raises a starting flag. Wide shot.

**Shot 5 — they stop, he doesn't**
> Mid-race on a city street. In the background, the rabbit stops to take selfies with fans and the cheetah dozes at a café table. In the foreground, Milo keeps sliding forward with a calm, focused smile, leaving a shiny trail.

**Shot 6 — the finish**
> Sunset at a finish line with a tape and confetti. Milo breaks the tape, eyes closed in joy, while the crowd cheers in shock and the cheetah and rabbit sprint up far behind. Golden light.

---

## 3. Animate each still (Seedance 2.5, 5 s, 9:16, start image = the still)

Add to every motion prompt: *Characters only move and react; they do not talk. Smooth camera, motion starts immediately.*

1. The camera tracks slowly alongside Milo at ground level as scooters and a drone whoosh past him.
2. The fox taps the watch and sighs; Milo gives a small sheepish smile and holds the parcel higher.
3. The camera tilts up from Milo to the giant banner; the laughing animals in the background point.
4. The frog drops the flag; the cheetah and rabbit explode off the line in a cloud of dust while Milo starts his slow slide.
5. The camera pushes in past the rabbit's selfie and the sleeping cheetah to Milo gliding steadily forward.
6. Milo breaks the finish tape in slow motion, confetti bursts, the crowd jumps to its feet.

---

## 4. Narration (one line per shot, same voice for all)

1. "Milo was the slowest courier in the whole city."
2. "Every delivery, he showed up last."
3. "So when he entered the biggest race in town, everyone laughed."
4. "The fast ones were gone in a second."
5. "But the fast ones kept stopping. Milo never did."
6. "Turns out, slow isn't the problem. Stopping is."

---

## 5. Put it together (CapCut)

1. Drop the six clips on the timeline in order.
2. Put each narration line under its clip.
3. Text → Auto captions, then pick a bold, one-word-at-a-time caption style.
4. Add soft background music at low volume, plus a whoosh on shot 4 and a cheer on shot 6.
5. Export 1080×1920 and turn on TikTok's "AI-generated" label when you post.
